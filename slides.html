FirstLease · SOM-centered rental planning01
THE PROBLEM

Finding a place is only the first step.

New international students may need to compare housing, interpret unfamiliar lease terms, coordinate move-in, and verify details with a landlord—all while settling into New Haven.

01
Compare

Which layout, rent, utilities, deal terms, and commute fit my needs?

02
Clarify

What does my lease actually say—and what should I ask?

03
Prepare

What should I do before move-in and key pickup?

Target user: an incoming Yale SOM renter who needs a practical, source-linked way to compare housing and organize lease-to-move-in questions.

The problem statement is a product hypothesis; the impact section identifies what still needs user validation.

FirstLease · Product positioning03
HOW IT WORKS

Two workflows, with the renter in control.

The housing assistant and planning tools do not call an LLM. Lease content is untrusted input; the renter checks each result against the original.

FirstLease · Impact evidence08
LIMITS & NEXT STEPS

A useful prototype stays honest about uncertainty.

Current limits
  • Dated housing snapshot, not live inventory
  • Some records are plan/pricing references, not vacancies
  • Phrase search can miss clauses; scanned PDFs are unsupported
  • Cost estimate depends on what the renter enters
  • No legal, property, or landlord verification
Next
  • Run a target-user task study
  • Measure lease extraction quality and token cost
  • Capture safety and normal-use evidence
  • Refresh listings only through permitted sources
  • Improve reminders after user feedback
